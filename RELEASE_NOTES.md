# Release Notes · 发行说明

## 261028.0

### Keep your screen bright during media playback

- On macOS 14.2 or later, automatic dimming pauses when supported media players or browsers use audio, including VLC, QuickTime Player, IINA, mpv, Infuse, Apple TV, Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Arc, Opera, Vivaldi and Zen.
- After audio output stops, the full waiting time starts again. You can still dim the screen manually, and Keep Computer Awake continues to work independently.
- A circular play marker in the menu bar and a status card below the settings make it clear when automatic dimming is paused. The status card is aligned with the settings and main button in all 12 languages.
- This follows audio activity: videos without audio may not be detected, and some apps may remain active after you pause. Safari, Apple Music and Spotify do not pause automatic dimming in this version. Earlier macOS versions keep their previous behavior.

### 播放媒体时暂缓调暗

- 在 macOS 14.2 或更新版本中，检测到受支持的播放器或浏览器使用音频时，会暂缓自动调暗。覆盖 VLC、QuickTime Player、IINA、mpv、Infuse、Apple TV，以及 Chrome、Edge、Firefox、Brave、Arc、Opera、Vivaldi 和 Zen。
- 音频输出停止后，重新等待你选好的完整时长。播放期间仍可手动调暗屏幕，保持电脑唤醒也可以独立使用。
- 菜单栏增加圆形播放标记，设置区下方显示清晰的暂停提示；提示与设置区、主按钮左右对齐，并覆盖全部 12 种语言。
- 这一判断依据音频活动：没有音轨的视频可能无法识别，部分应用暂停后仍可能保持活跃。本版暂不覆盖 Safari，Apple Music 和 Spotify 也不会暂停自动调暗。较早的 macOS 版本保持原有行为。

## 261023.0

### Faster brightness restoration

- Moving the mouse or pressing a key while the screen is dimming now stops dimming and restores the original brightness.
- Responds faster when you return to a dimmed screen, including when using multiple displays.

### 更快恢复屏幕亮度

- 屏幕正在变暗时，移动鼠标或按键会停止调暗并恢复原亮度。
- 屏幕调暗后，回来操作时恢复更快，也适用于多块显示器。

## 261021.0

### Clearer keep-awake settings

- Clearly separates the keep-awake explanation from the reminder that your screen still dims at the chosen time.
- Updates this wording and layout in all 12 languages.

### 更清楚的保持唤醒说明

- 将保持唤醒的用途与调暗提醒分开显示，明确开启后屏幕仍会按设定调暗。
- 同步更新全部 12 种语言的说明与排版。

## 261019.0

### First release

- Automatically check for and download updates for direct installations.
- Dim the screen when you step away, then restore brightness with a mouse movement or keypress.
- Remember your choice to keep the computer awake, and restore it when Doze opens. This also prevents automatic sleep during downloads and exports.
- Enjoy day and night artwork, Liquid Glass buttons, a clearer app icon and more room for the landscape.
- Choose from 12 languages. Activity information stays on your Mac.

### 首次发布

- 直接下载版现在会自动检查并下载更新。
- 离开电脑后自动调暗屏幕，回来动动鼠标或按键就恢复亮度。
- 记住你保持电脑唤醒的选择，并在 Doze 打开时恢复；下载和导出期间也不会因自动睡眠而中断。
- 提供柔和的昼夜外观、液态玻璃按钮、更清晰的应用图标，以及更舒展的树林背景。
- 支持 12 种语言，活动信息只留在本机。
